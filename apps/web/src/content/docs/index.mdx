## Why this exists

Coworking operators are flooded with SaaS demos, “awesome lists,” and vendor PDFs. Few resources carefully separate **OSI open source** from **source-available**, **open-core**, **freemium SaaS**, and **free trials**—or explain what a small space can actually run without a full-time platform team.

Open Coworking Stack is a **documentation-first, Git-versioned catalog** with:

- verified-where-possible metadata (and honest `unverified` gaps);
- coworking workflows, roles, and problems—not generic IT categories alone;
- implementation guides and Docker Compose starter packs where licensing permits;
- comparison pages and low-cost reference stacks.

## Critical boundaries

1. **We do not vendor upstream projects.** Official repos stay authoritative.
2. **We do not mislabel licenses.** Source-visible ≠ open source.
3. **We do not invent pricing or star counts.** Missing facts stay `unverified`.
4. **Software does not replace staff.** It may reduce or augment tasks with human oversight.
