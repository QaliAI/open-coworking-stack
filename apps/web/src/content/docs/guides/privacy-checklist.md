---
title: Privacy checklist
description: Practical privacy questions for coworking operators adopting software.
---

This is not legal advice. Use it to prepare conversations with counsel and vendors.

## Data map (one page)

- What personal data do we collect? (members, visitors, staff, leads)
- Where is it stored? (which tools, which regions)
- Who can access it? (roles, vendors, integrators)
- How long do we keep it?
- How do people request export/deletion?
- What happens on vendor offboarding?

## High-risk patterns

- CCTV without signage/policy
- Member “relationship CRMs” without transparency
- Public calendars exposing personal meeting titles
- Chat logs retained forever
- Analytics without consent where required
- Shared staff logins (no audit trail)

## Prefer

- Data minimization
- Role-based access
- Short retention for video and raw logs
- Privacy-friendly analytics when marketing metrics suffice
- Documented subprocessors for SaaS
