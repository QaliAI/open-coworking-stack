description: Lead capture through tour and conversion.
---

## Stages

1. **Capture** — forms/chat into CRM with spam control.
2. **Tour** — Cal.com/Easy!Appointments + show-up reminders.
3. **Close** — proposal, negotiation, win/loss reasons.

## Metrics

- Response time to first touch
- Lead→tour and tour→member conversion
- No-show rate

## Common failure

Marketing captures leads into a personal inbox nobody else sees. Use a shared inbox or CRM from day one.
