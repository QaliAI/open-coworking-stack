## Stages

1. **Agreement & identity** — e-sign + CRM; legal review of templates.
2. **Provision** — access, Wi-Fi, portal, lockers; checklist tickets.
3. **Billing start** — first invoice/subscription; payment failure path.
4. **Welcome** — human welcome plus optional automated emails.

## Suggested tooling mix

- Vertical SaaS (Cobot/OfficeRnD/Nexudus) **or** CRM + invoicing DIY
- DocuSeal/Documenso for agreements (jurisdiction review)
- FreeScout/Chatwoot for questions
- BookStack for orientation FAQ

## Human oversight

Software can sequence tasks; judgment on exceptions, discounts, and cultural welcome remains human.
