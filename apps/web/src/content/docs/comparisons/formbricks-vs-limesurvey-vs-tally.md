## Shared dimensions

| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------- |
| [Formbricks](../../tools/generated/formbricks/) | Product/experience feedback and surveys    | Partial                   |

| Tally                                           | Managed, low-friction forms                | Partial; no catalog entry |

## Operator profiles

Formbricks merits evaluation for feedback embedded in a digital journey, LimeSurvey for complex
research instruments, and Tally for fast managed forms. The operator's privacy, branching,
accessibility, identity, and analysis needs should decide the lane.

## Task coverage

They can augment listening surveys, event feedback, service requests, applications, and structured
intake. Survey design, consent, safeguarding, response interpretation, and action planning remain
human work.

## Human work remaining

People define a legitimate purpose, minimize questions, review accessibility, protect anonymity,
interpret bias, triage urgent responses, and report what changed.

## Technical difficulty

Tally is low infrastructure effort. Self-hosting Formbricks or LimeSurvey is moderate and adds
identity, email, database, storage, patching, backup, and analytics responsibilities.

## True-cost categories

Count subscription or hosting, response/storage growth, email, identity, implementation, design,
analysis, integrations, support, privacy review, backups, and retention work. Exact prices and free
limits are not established.

## Portability

Export questions, branching, translations, response values, timestamps, consent metadata, files,
labels, and codebooks. A response CSV without the instrument is incomplete.

## Integrations and verification caveat

Embedding, email, webhook, CRM, analytics, and identity claims are `partial` until edition, data
direction, field mapping, retries, respondent identity, and deletion are tested against official docs.

## Security

Avoid collecting unnecessary sensitive data, separate anonymous and identified surveys, restrict
exports, protect uploads, define retention, verify public-form abuse controls, patch self-hosted
instances, and test deletion.

## Licensing

Formbricks and LimeSurvey publish self-hostable source under their current project terms; verify
edition boundaries and current licenses. Tally is proprietary SaaS.

## Maintenance

Maintain templates, ownership, consent text, routing, exports, integrations, retention jobs,
accessibility checks, upgrades, backups, and a process for urgent responses.

## Scenario recommendations

Pilot a short member survey and an operational intake form. Test mobile/keyboard use, branching,
anonymous handling, duplicate submission, export, deletion, and an urgent-response route.

## Do not use when

Do not collect data without a decision purpose, promise anonymity while retaining identifiers, or
use a generic form as an emergency, payment, identity-proofing, or access-control system.

## Verification

Verified on **2026-07-18**. Features, licenses, prices, free limits, and integrations are `partial` or
`not established` where no current evidence is retained.
