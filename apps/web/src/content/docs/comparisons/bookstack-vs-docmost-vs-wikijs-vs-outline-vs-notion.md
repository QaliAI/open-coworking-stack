## Operator profiles

BookStack suits teams that value a clear hierarchy; Docmost and Outline merit collaborative-editor
pilots; Wiki.js merits evaluation where wiki structure and technical administration fit; Notion
suits teams prioritizing managed-service adoption. Validate with frontline staff, not only authors.

## Task coverage

All candidates can augment SOP publishing, onboarding, shift references, incident playbooks, and
multi-site knowledge. None ensures content is correct, approved, read, or followed.

## Human work remaining

People author, approve, review, archive, train, handle exceptions, and verify that policy matches
practice. Assign owners and review dates to operationally critical pages.

## Technical difficulty

Notion is low infrastructure effort. Self-hosted options are moderate and require identity, email,
storage, backup, upgrade, search, and attachment management. Migration can be high effort.

## True-cost categories

Count subscription or hosting, paid editions, migration, identity, storage, backups, support,
administration, authoring, review time, training, and exit cleanup. Exact prices and free limits are
not established.

## Portability

Test hierarchical export and re-import of pages, links, tables, attachments, comments, permissions,
history, stable identifiers, and redirects. Markdown/HTML export alone may lose behavior.

## Integrations and verification caveat

SSO, directory, import/export, API, embedding, and automation claims are `partial` until current
edition, permissions, field fidelity, limits, and round-trip behavior are tested against official
docs.

## Security

Separate public/member/staff content, use least privilege, review shared links, protect incident and
security procedures, remove departed users, define retention, patch self-hosted systems, and restore
test attachments.

## Licensing

BookStack and Wiki.js are open source; Docmost's and Outline's current terms and edition boundaries
must be checked; Outline is source-available rather than OSI open source; Notion is proprietary SaaS.

## Maintenance

Budget for content lifecycle work as well as software work: ownership, review queues, broken links,
search quality, permission audits, upgrades, backups, and archive policy.

## Scenario recommendations

Migrate ten representative SOPs, including a table, image, attachment, restricted page, and revision.
Have a new staff member find and use them, then test export and restore.

## Do not use when

Do not use a wiki as a substitute for training, approval, incident command, or a transactional
system. Avoid a platform whose export cannot preserve your critical document structure.
