## Shared dimensions

## Operator profiles

Listmonk suits technical teams needing focused self-hosted campaigns; Brevo or MailerLite merit
evaluation when managed delivery and simpler operations matter; Mautic suits teams prepared to own
broader campaign automation. Deliverability requirements can outweigh software preference.

## Task coverage

Candidates can augment newsletters, segmented updates, approved campaigns, preferences, and campaign
reporting. Consent basis, audience selection, message approval, and member-service communications
remain operator responsibilities.

## Human work remaining

People define lawful audiences, write and approve content, handle sensitive events, monitor replies,
review suppression, investigate complaints, and decide campaign changes.

## Technical difficulty

Managed SaaS is low-to-moderate configuration. Self-hosted applications are moderate-to-high because
mail delivery, domain authentication, reputation, queues, bounces, complaints, and upgrades remain.

## True-cost categories

Count subscription or hosting, delivery provider, contact/message growth, templates, migration,
domain setup, consent cleanup, support, monitoring, staff production time, and incident response.
Exact prices and free limits are not established.

## Portability

Export contacts, consent source/time, suppression and bounce states, segments, custom fields,
templates, campaign metadata, and reports. Never migrate only the active subscriber list.

## Integrations and verification caveat

CRM, forms, SMTP/API, webhook, analytics, and automation claims are `partial` until edition, consent
fields, direction, retries, suppression behavior, and current official docs are verified in a pilot.

## Security

Restrict bulk-send and export privileges, require strong administrator authentication, protect API
keys, separate transactional and campaign use where appropriate, minimize profile data, and retain
consent evidence.

## Licensing

Listmonk and Mautic are open-source projects with self-hosting obligations; Brevo and MailerLite are
proprietary SaaS. Verify current repository licenses, provider terms, and data-processing terms.

## Maintenance

Maintain domain authentication, reputation, bounce/complaint handling, suppression lists, templates,
consent sync, integrations, backups, upgrades, and a send-approval checklist.

## Scenario recommendations

Pilot a small, permissioned audience with subscription, preference change, unsubscribe, hard bounce,
complaint handling, reply routing, and export. Select on safe list hygiene and operational clarity.

## Do not use when

Do not upload contacts without a documented basis, send critical emergency communications through an
unproven campaign path, or self-host mail solely to avoid a provider fee.

## Verification

Verified on **2026-07-18**. Prices, free limits, deliverability outcomes, feature depth, and
integrations remain `partial` or `not established`.
