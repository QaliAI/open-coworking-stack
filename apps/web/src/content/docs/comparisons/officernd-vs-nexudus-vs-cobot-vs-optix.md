## Operator profiles

All four merit consideration by operators wanting a managed vertical platform. The best fit depends
on location count, operating model, geography, accounting process, member experience, and available
implementation support; relative fit is not established without that profile.

## Task coverage

Official positioning supports evaluating membership, booking, billing, member-facing, and operational
workflows. Exact depth by edition is `partial`; use the [capability benchmark](../commercial-platform-capability-benchmark/)
to record observed evidence rather than converting marketing pages into yes/no claims.

## Human work remaining

Teams still own deal approval, contract review, billing and tax exceptions, failed payments, access
exceptions, member support, data quality, and reconciliation with finance systems.

## Technical difficulty

Configuration is moderate. Migration, identity, payment, accounting, access-control, and custom
integration work can be high. Require the implementation party to identify dependencies and owners.

## True-cost categories

Include subscription and add-ons, implementation, migration, payment processing, accounting and
access connectors, messaging, support, training, sandbox needs, custom reports, annual increases,
and exit assistance. Exact price and free limits are not established here.

## Portability

Test full and incremental export of members, companies, plans, contracts, invoices, payments,
bookings, credits, access mappings, notes, and attachments. API availability is not equivalent to a
complete exit path.

## Integrations and verification caveat

Named integrations are `partial` until the selected edition, region, data direction, fields,
authentication, limits, retries, deletion, and support ownership are confirmed in official docs and
a sandbox. Do not infer parity among these products from partner logos.

## Security

Request current security and privacy documents through each vendor's official process. Validate SSO
and role requirements, administrator auditability, subprocessor governance, retention, incident
notification, and account termination; no certification claim is made here.

## Licensing

These are proprietary managed SaaS products. Contract rights, data-processing terms, API terms,
service levels, and export assistance matter more than source-code availability.

## Maintenance

The vendor operates the service, while the operator maintains configuration, roles, templates,
connectors, data quality, release acceptance, and continuity procedures.

## Scenario recommendations

Shortlist all vendors that support your geography and operating model, then score the same real
workflow and export test. Prefer the smallest acceptable customization footprint and clearest owner
for failed cross-system transactions.

## Do not use when

Do not select from a generic demo, an unpriced requirements list, or an integration logo sheet. Do
not use a vertical suite when a simple bounded tool is sufficient or when contract/export terms fail
your exit requirements.
