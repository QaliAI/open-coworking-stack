## Shared dimensions

| Tool                                          | Evaluation lane                                   | Evidence status |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | --------------- |
| [Documenso](../../tools/generated/documenso/) | Self-hostable/cloud electronic-signature workflow | Partial         |
| [DocuSeal](../../tools/generated/docuseal/)   | Self-hostable/cloud electronic-signature workflow | Partial         |

Compare template preparation, signer experience, evidence, authentication, reminders, completion,
download, API, retention, and administrator controls for the intended jurisdiction and document.

## Operator profiles

Either may merit a pilot for standard, counsel-approved agreements where the operator can own
deployment and evidence handling. Jurisdiction, document type, risk, and support requirements should
drive the choice.

## Task coverage

They can augment document preparation, signature requests, reminders, status tracking, and completed
document collection. They do not provide legal advice, negotiate terms, verify every signer, or
decide enforceability.

## Human work remaining

People approve templates and counterparties, handle accommodations and exceptions, review completion,
store records correctly, respond to disputes, and obtain legal advice where required.

## Technical difficulty

Managed paths are moderate configuration; self-hosting is moderate-to-high due to email delivery,
storage, identity, key/secret handling, updates, backups, and evidentiary continuity.

## True-cost categories

Count subscription or hosting, email, storage, paid editions, template setup, migration, identity,
support, legal review, audit retention, backups, upgrades, and dispute handling. Exact prices and
free limits are not established.

## Portability

Export originals, completed documents, evidence/audit records, timestamps, signer metadata,
templates, identifiers, and integrity information. Test whether exported evidence remains intelligible
without the service.

## Integrations and verification caveat

Storage, email, CRM, API, webhook, identity, and embedded-signing claims are `partial` until current
edition, authentication, event delivery, retries, evidence fields, and deletion are verified in
official docs and a sandbox.

## Security

Protect documents and signing links, minimize signer data, separate template and send permissions,
review administrators, secure email and storage, log access, define retention, patch self-hosted
instances, and restore test evidence.

## Licensing

Both publish self-hostable source with commercial offerings. Verify current repository licenses,
enterprise feature boundaries, trademark terms, and hosted-service conditions.

## Maintenance

Maintain templates, legal approval dates, sender accounts, email delivery, webhooks, storage,
retention, upgrades, backups, evidence exports, and a manual signing fallback.

## Scenario recommendations

Pilot one low-risk approved document from preparation through signature, decline, correction,
expiration, export, verification, and restore. Have counsel or the responsible owner review evidence.

## Do not use when

Do not infer legal validity, identity assurance, certification, or jurisdictional acceptance from
self-hosting or a feature label. Do not automate sending unapproved or negotiable terms.

## Verification

Verified on **2026-07-18**. Legal suitability, identity assurance, feature depth, prices, free limits,
and integrations are `partial` or `not established`.
