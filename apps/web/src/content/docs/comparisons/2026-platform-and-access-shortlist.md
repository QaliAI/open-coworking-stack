**Reviewed: November 11, 2026.** This is a **research shortlist**, not a best-to-worst ranking, endorsement, purchase recommendation or proof that integrations work. High-level product positions were checked against the vendors' own pages. Vendor claims are not independent evidence of software quality or plan entitlements. Re-check the exact geography, contract, price and selected edition before buying.

## Start with the decision, not a vendor

Ask **what problem needs fixing in the next 30 days**:

- **Billing, memberships, contracts and reporting are scattered:** evaluate a dedicated coworking platform first. Adding a separate point solution may make the problem worse.
- **The current membership system works, but room booking is painful:** evaluate your existing system's booking module before a specialist scheduler.
- **Members are locked out or former members retain access:** evaluate access control and the actual provisioning/revocation workflow; don't treat a booking app as an access system.
- **A tiny space has no technical owner:** favor a supported hosted system; a collection of "free" self-hosted applications can cost far more in maintenance.
- **Multi-site operators have inconsistent reporting:** insist on location-scoped identifiers, invoice reconciliation, shared roles, and a usable cross-location export.

**No technology purchase is the correct outcome** if a process or configuration change solves the problem using the tools already in place.

## Four operator profiles — and what to test

| Operator | First priority | Pilot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single location, owner at front desk | One owner, simple training, accessible support, clear export and total price | Run a day-pass, a room booking, a recurring invoice and a cancellation with fictional records |
| Sales-led private-office location | Lead-to-tour-to-contract continuity and suite availability | Run a sourced inquiry, tour, proposal, signed agreement, deposit/billing and CRM reconciliation |
| Multi-location group | Cross-site permissions, portfolio metrics, accounting accuracy and migration | Create the same product in two sites, move a test member, reconcile reports and confirm permissions |
| Virtual office / mail-heavy operator | Package chain of custody, pickup authority, partner billing and member privacy | Log a fictional delivery, notify, verify pickup, reconcile partner invoicing and test exceptions |

## The same scripted trial for every shortlisted **full coworking suite**

Do not use a polished vendor demo as the acceptance test. In the selected contract tier, ask the implementation team to:

1. Create a fictional company, an individual member and a prospect; preserve their distinct record IDs.
2. Capture a lead, log a tour and quote an actual available test product; verify lead attribution survives conversion.
3. Generate a test contract and recurring membership invoice; check proration, a failed payment, refunds and tax review without charging a real card.
4. Book the same meeting room from two sessions; verify conflict resolution, credits, cancellation and calendar time zones.
5. Assign, then revoke, a fictional member's access using the **actual proposed hardware/connector** in a safe test environment. Include expired-membership and manual-exception cases.
6. Test onboarding, role permissions, support requests, an access problem, and a member exit.
7. Export members, companies, agreements, invoice details, payments, credits, bookings, identifiers and attachments. **Open the exported files** and reconcile counts and totals.
8. Demonstrate a vendor outage procedure, data restoration or fallback, and a named support escalation owner.

Mark each result **observed pass / observed fail / not tested**, with the edition, date, approver and evidence reference. Vendor statements, comparison articles and integration badges are supporting research—not observed passes.

## Total cost: ask for one comparable quote

Compare the cost of the **same job** over 12 months and include:

- Subscription price at the operator's real location, member and resource counts
- Mandatory onboarding, migration, implementation, staff training and premium support
- White-label app, calendar, accounting, automation, API, integration and network add-ons
- Payment-processing and communication charges
- Door controllers, readers, installation, credentials, internet/network uptime and replacements where applicable
- Internal administration, reconciliation, backups, incident response and offboarding/exit

If a line item is unknown, write **unknown—quote needed**; do not invent a number or imply it is free.

## What makes a vendor comparison trustworthy?

Use the [shared capability worksheet](../commercial-platform-capability-benchmark/) and require proof of: actual edition, use case, data direction, fields, stable identifiers, time zone, tax and refund behavior, retry policy, revocation, human approval, audit trail, error alerts, portability and response when the vendor is down.

**Decision rule:** shortlist no more than three plausible products for the actual operator profile; run an identical scripted test; choose only after reviewing price, staff burden, privacy, building access safety and data exit. A narrow successful pilot beats a long feature table.
