---
title: When to obtain professional help
description: Stop conditions for legal, financial, privacy, security, access, safety, and regulated operations.
---

This project provides technical and operational starting points, not professional advice. Stop the
affected rollout and obtain qualified help before:

| Area                                   | Trigger                                                                                                                  | Reviewer                                                                      |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Contracts and consumer rights          | Membership terms, waivers, renewal/cancellation, deposits, surveillance terms, or exceptions                             | Lawyer qualified locally                                                      |
| Privacy and data protection            | Identity, video/biometrics, access history, accessibility/health details, monitoring, cross-border data, or AI profiling | Privacy counsel or qualified data-protection lead                             |
| Postal and virtual office              | Receiving, opening, scanning, forwarding, destroying, or releasing mail and customer verification                        | Postal/CMRA or local regulatory specialist and counsel                        |
| Accounting and tax                     | Chart/tax choices, corrections, recognition, deposits, credits, write-offs, payroll, or statutory reports                | Qualified accountant or tax adviser                                           |
| Payments                               | Card-data scope, payment-flow changes, mandates, disputes, or marketplace funds                                          | Provider and PCI-qualified help as needed                                     |
| Cybersecurity and identity             | Public exposure, SSO/MFA, privilege, incidents, encryption/key custody, or high-impact migration                         | Qualified security practitioner                                               |
| Physical access and safety             | Locks, elevators, alarms, emergency systems, capacity, cameras, or visitor denial                                        | Licensed access/fire/building professionals, insurer, authority as applicable |
| Accessibility                          | Member portals, physical routes, event accommodations, or claimed conformance                                            | Accessibility specialist and affected users                                   |
| Employment and monitoring              | Hiring, scheduling, scoring, sanctions, termination, checks, tracking, or surveillance                                   | Employment counsel and HR professional                                        |
| Insurance, property, and regulated use | Liability assumptions, occupancy, subletting, events, food/alcohol, childcare, health, or regulated tenants              | Broker, counsel, landlord, and authorities as applicable                      |

Software and agents must not make final decisions about member eligibility, contracts, pricing
exceptions, credit, refunds, debt, identity, consent, access exceptions, emergencies, employment,
accommodations, retention, disclosure, or incident closure. Keep a named human approver, reversible
decision record, manual route, and disable switch.

When escalating, preserve evidence, minimize shared data, state jurisdiction and assumptions, obtain
written scoped advice, update controls/training, and re-test with synthetic data. Vendor product help is
not automatically independent legal, financial, security, privacy, accessibility, or safety assurance.
