---
title: Maintenance cadence
description: Suggested weekly and monthly maintenance for operator stacks.
---

## Weekly

- Review Uptime Kuma incidents
- Apply urgent security updates
- Check backup job success
- Clear spam from shared inboxes

## Monthly

- Restore drill for one app
- Access review (departed staff/contractors)
- Dependency updates for Compose images
- Export SaaS membership data

## Quarterly

- License/pricing re-verification for critical tools
- Privacy retention review
- Disaster recovery tabletop (2 hours)
