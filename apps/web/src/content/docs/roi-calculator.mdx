<Aside type="caution" title="Time value is not automatically cash savings">
  Use measured workflow volume and time. Capacity that becomes available has value only when it can
  be redirected to useful work; it is not a staffing reduction or guaranteed financial return.
</Aside>

This browser-only estimator shows the assumptions and formulas needed for an operator conversation.
Use the low/base/high range, then validate it with a bounded pilot and actual invoices and time samples.

<RoiCalculator />

## Formula boundaries

- Potential hours redirected = adopted task volume × positive minutes difference ÷ 60.
- Gross labor capacity value = potential hours redirected × loaded hourly labor cost.
- Monthly operating cost includes software/hosting, other cash cost, and maintenance labor.
- Net estimates subtract operating and setup costs; they do not assert that cash will be saved.
- Payback is not shown when the estimated monthly net value is zero or negative.
