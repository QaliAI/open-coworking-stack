---
title: Navigate by budget
description: Five monthly cash bands with explicit labor, setup, delivery, hosting, and exit warnings.
---

These are planning bands for recurring **software and service cash**, not price promises or total cost.
Replace every assumption with current official quotes in your currency and jurisdiction.

> **Labor warning:** free software and near-zero license spend do not make implementation, data cleanup,
> training, administration, hosting, storage, email/SMS, security, backups, restore drills, incidents,
> upgrades, professional review, or migration free. If staff hours are not budgeted, the plan is
> incomplete.

## 1. Near-zero cash

Use free/open software or existing entitlements while explicitly accepting manual work and skilled
maintenance. This band is unsuitable when recovery ownership is absent.

- Review [Near-Zero Cash Technical Self-Host](../../stacks/generated/bootstrap-near-zero/).
- Include a host, domain, off-host backup, delivery services, and loaded labor even when license cash is
  zero.

## 2. Under 50 per month

Keep one bounded workflow or a very small managed/self-hosted mix. Currency is intentionally not fixed;
local taxes and exchange rates apply.

- Use the [Stack Builder](../../stack-builder/) with current quotes.
- Reject any combination that relies on expiring trials or unmeasured overages.

## 3. Under 200 per month

Allow a small operator stack with clearer support or a few managed services, while retaining explicit
systems of record and an exit path.

- Review [Under 100 Monthly](../../stacks/generated/under-100-monthly/) as a narrower planning target.
- Compare [Under 250 Hybrid](../../stacks/generated/under-250-hybrid/) before assuming the broader band
  is sufficient.

## 4. Under 500 per month

Budget for a broader hosted/hybrid arrangement, delivery usage, protected storage, and recurring
administration. More tools are not automatically a better stack.

- Compare [Commercial Hybrid](../../stacks/generated/commercial-hybrid/).
- Preserve budget for staff training, reconciliation, independent backup/export, and support.

## 5. Flexible or custom

Use when requirements, site count, support, hardware, usage, or risk drive the decision more than a
fixed cap. Flexible does not mean unbounded.

- Model low, expected, and high five-year cash and labor in the
  [buy/build/self-host guide](../../ops/buy-build-self-host/).
- Set approval thresholds, contingency, contract/renewal dates, and a funded exit scenario.

For every band, state monthly cash, setup labor, maintenance labor, hosting/storage, messaging/usage,
payment fees, hardware, professional review, incident allowance, and exit cost separately. Do not claim
savings until a measured baseline and post-pilot outcome exist.
