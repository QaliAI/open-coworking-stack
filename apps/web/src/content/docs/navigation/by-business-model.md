---
title: Navigate by business model
description: Seven coworking and flexible-workspace operating models with distinct workflow and risk needs.
---

Most operators combine models. Select the primary revenue/service promise first, then add only the
workflows with named owners, capacity, professional review, and tested fallback.

## 1. Membership coworking

Shared workspace sold through recurring memberships, day passes, and community programming.

- Start with [Member Experience Lite](../../stacks/generated/member-experience-lite/).
- Prioritize onboarding, desk/room booking, community support, billing, and offboarding.
- Watch capacity, inclusion, access exceptions, and over-automation of human community work.

## 2. Private offices and serviced workspace

Dedicated rooms or suites with recurring service, access, facilities, and commercial commitments.

- Compare [Space, Room, and Desk Operations](../../stacks/generated/space-room-desk-operations/).
- Prioritize inventory, proposals, agreements, access, maintenance, invoices, and renewals.
- Obtain local review for lease/license structure, deposits, occupancy, and building obligations.

## 3. Hybrid flexible workspace

A deliberate mix of memberships, private offices, day passes, rooms, and hosted/commercial services.

- Compare the [Commercial Hybrid](../../stacks/generated/commercial-hybrid/) stack.
- Make product, customer, booking, invoice, payment, and access systems of record explicit.
- Avoid accidental tool sprawl and product rules that staff cannot explain or reconcile.

## 4. Meeting and event venue

Rooms or larger spaces sold for meetings, training, community events, or external programs.

- Start with [Meeting Room and Event Revenue](../../stacks/generated/meeting-room-event-revenue/).
- Prioritize fit/risk assessment, booking, vendors, registration, access, run-of-show, billing, and
  closeout.
- Keep life safety, capacity, accessibility, alcohol/food, insurance, and local permissions human-led.

## 5. Virtual office and mailroom

Address, receipt, custody, notification, release, scan, and forwarding services for approved clients.

- Start with [Virtual Office and Mailroom](../../stacks/generated/virtual-office-mailroom/).
- Prioritize identity/authorization, custody evidence, privacy, billing, unclaimed items, and
  continuity.
- Do not launch until postal/CMRA or equivalent local obligations receive qualified review.

## 6. Incubator, accelerator, or innovation hub

Workspace combined with cohorts, mentoring, programs, partner reporting, and events.

- Combine [community programming](../../workflows/generated/community-programming/) with
  [event lifecycle](../../workflows/generated/event-lifecycle/).
- Separate member service records from applications, judging, funding, protected characteristics, and
  confidential venture data.
- Keep selection, funding, conflicts, and participant welfare decisions with accountable people.

## 7. Multi-location, network, or franchise operation

Multiple sites share some brand, systems, standards, vendors, or reporting while retaining local
operating duties.

- Evaluate [Multi-Location Operator](../../stacks/generated/multi-location-operator/).
- Define global versus local authority, site exceptions, support coverage, data sharing, and staged
  rollout.
- Do not publish franchise-private data or assume a central dashboard removes local safety, privacy,
  employment, property, or service accountability.
