Open Coworking Stack is an independent, community-maintained technical resource.

Nothing in this repository is legal advice, tax advice, accounting advice, security certification, or a compliance guarantee. Operators remain responsible for their own licensing decisions, privacy obligations, accessibility requirements, payment handling, employment practices, insurance, and local regulations.

## Not a vendor endorsement

Inclusion of a tool does **not** mean:

- the project endorses the vendor;
- the tool is free of risk;
- the free tier is permanent;
- the license classification is a legal opinion;
- the tool is suitable for every jurisdiction or business model.

## Metadata can become stale

Pricing, free-tier limits, license terms, security posture, and feature sets change. Each catalog entry includes verification dates and confidence notes. Always re-check official sources before production decisions.

## Implementation risk remains yours

Docker Compose starter packs, deployment recipes, and guides are starting points. They are not turnkey production systems. Harden authentication, backups, monitoring, TLS, secrets management, and data retention for your environment before serving members or handling payments.

## Human judgment required

Software may reduce repetitive tasks. It does not replace community managers, sales staff, facilities teams, or operators. Automation can create new failure modes. Design for human oversight.
