# BigBlueButton coworking evaluation stub

> This is an incomplete evaluation stub, not an implementation guide.

## Verified snapshot

BigBlueButton is an OSI-approved `LGPL-3.0-only` virtual-classroom platform with breakouts, whiteboards, and recordings. The coworking case is workshops and training. Self-hosting is expert-level and resource-intensive; a commercial host may be the lower-risk experiment. It holds session metadata, recordings, chat, and personal data.

Sources reviewed **2026-07-17**: [homepage](https://bigbluebutton.org/), [repository](https://github.com/bigbluebutton/bigbluebutton), and [documentation](https://docs.bigbluebutton.org/).

## Evaluation questions

- Are classroom features materially better for the planned workshop than a simpler meeting tool?
- What capacity, moderator training, recording consent, and retention are required?
- Can recordings and chat be exported and deleted on schedule?
- Is managed hosting preferable to expert self-hosting?

## Smallest experiment

Use a one-month managed host for one workshop series before considering self-hosting. Test attendee access, moderator controls, recording consent, export, and deletion.

## Risks

- Infrastructure sizing and event-day reliability
- Recording/chat privacy and consent
- Operational complexity and unverified hosting cost/support
