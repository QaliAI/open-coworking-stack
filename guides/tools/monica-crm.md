# Monica coworking evaluation stub

> This is an incomplete evaluation stub, not an implementation guide.

## Verified snapshot

Monica is an OSI-approved `AGPL-3.0-or-later` self-hosted personal CRM. The catalog warns against using it for opaque member dossiers and suggests a vendor/partner pilot first. It holds contact notes, reminders, activities, and personal data.

Sources reviewed **2026-07-17**: [homepage](https://www.monicahq.com/), [repository](https://github.com/monicahq/monica), and [wiki](https://github.com/monicahq/monica/wiki).

## Evaluation questions

- Is the purpose transparent and proportionate, especially for member data?
- Which note types are prohibited and how are people informed?
- Who handles access, correction, deletion, retention, and export?
- Is a conventional vendor CRM or shared register more appropriate?

## Smallest experiment

Pilot only with a few vendor/partner relationships and synthetic or approved notes. Do not create member dossiers. Test data minimization, access, export, and deletion.

## Risks

- High privacy and trust risk from subjective relationship notes
- Data minimization, correction, and deletion failures
- Product scope may not fit organizational CRM governance
