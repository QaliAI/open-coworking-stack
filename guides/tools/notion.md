# Notion for coworking operations knowledge

This guide scopes managed Notion SaaS to five non-critical coworking SOPs in one private operations home. It is documentation review and a pilot plan, not evidence that a workspace, export, or recovery has been runtime-tested.

## 1. Coworking outcome

Help staff find current opening, closing, tour, room-reset, and escalation instructions while making content ownership visible. Notion augments documentation and coordination; it does not validate instructions or replace operational judgment.

## 2. Pilot scope

Copy five approved SOPs, assign an owner/review date to each, test staff permissions, give one synthetic guest access to a separate test page, revoke that guest, and reconstruct a usable fallback from export. Keep source documents authoritative.

## 3. Fit

**Good fit:** a team needing collaboratively maintained SOPs, meeting notes, and lightweight coordination, with active permission, guest, content, and export governance.

## 4. Non-fit and alternatives

**Poor fit:** self-hosting requirements; legal, tax, contract, identity, access-control, or durable-record archives; member/billing databases; confidential casework; or unowned pages/links. Keep the controlled file store or evaluate a self-hosted knowledge base when export, residency, or SaaS control is unacceptable.

## 5. Coworking use cases

- Private staff operations home for non-critical procedures.
- Meeting notes and action logs with named owners.
- Lightweight project trackers whose source-of-truth and fallback are explicit.
- A separate, narrowly shared contractor orientation page.

Payment credentials, door codes, member files, contracts, and safety authority are excluded.

## 6. Tasks reduced or augmented

Notion can reduce duplicate SOP copies, repeated document searching, and manual review reminders. It cannot decide whether an SOP is safe/correct, approve publication or access, reconcile business systems, or guarantee archive-grade recovery.

## 7. Human decisions and approvals

SOP owners approve content; managers approve guests; privacy approves data classes; workspace owners approve permission/integration changes; operations approves rollout; and an independent reviewer signs export/fallback results. AI output remains a draft pending owner review.

## 8. Owners and RACI

Name an operations sponsor, knowledge owner, owner for each page/tracker, workspace administrator, privacy reviewer, support/recovery owner, and independent export reviewer. Do not let one admin silently approve and verify their own external-sharing exception.

## 9. Prerequisites

Inventory source versions and attachments, define staff/guest groups, prohibited data, content review interval, approved export destination, account recovery, support route, and prior staff index. Do not migrate an ownerless page.

## 10. Skills and effort boundary

The pilot needs workspace information architecture, permission/privacy review, content migration, spreadsheet/CSV inspection, and change-management skills. Software setup is easy; ongoing review, export reconciliation, and cleanup create continuing labor. No implementation-time promise is verified.

## 11. Deployment choice

Notion is proprietary managed SaaS with free and paid plans, not self-hosted software. Select the plan only after checking current member/guest, export, history, security, AI, support, and billing boundaries on official pages. Provider runtime/backups remain provider-controlled.

## 12. Architecture and dependencies

Map named accounts and recovery, owners/groups/teamspaces, private operations home, external guest area, connected apps, identity path if used, and protected export storage outside Notion. Do not publish internal pages to the web or make critical SOP availability depend on one SaaS login.

## 13. License and edition boundary

Notion is proprietary freemium SaaS. Plan features and commercial terms change; verify official pricing and contract terms. This guide does not establish entitlement to SSO, SCIM, audit, retention, granular permissions, support, residency, or any AI control.

## 14. Total cost boundary

Annual true cost = paid members/add-ons + migration and workspace design + owner reviews + access/guest administration + export storage/reconciliation + integration review + training/support + incident and exit labor. Record cash and loaded labor separately with low/base/high cases; unknown is not zero.

## 15. Data map

| Data            | Source                  | Notion purpose            | Authority                 | Exit artifact              |
| --------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Five SOP pages  | Controlled source files | Staff reference           | Source files during pilot | HTML and Markdown          |
| Light tracker   | Staff                   | Non-critical coordination | Named tracker owner       | CSV and field dictionary   |
| Comments/files  | Staff/approved guest    | Context                   | Page owner                | HTML/files where supported |
| Accounts/guests | Admin process           | Access                    | Admin register            | Offboarding record         |

Pages, comments, files, and account details may be personal data. Excluded data must not enter test pages, AI, integrations, or exports.

## 16. Privacy and retention

Classify each page `internal`, `external-test`, or `prohibited`; minimize personal data; set review/deletion rules by class; and protect exports like source content. Trash/history behavior is plan/setting-dependent and is not a retention policy. Verify contractual location, subprocessors, and privacy terms before sensitive use.

## 17. Security baseline

Use named accounts, strong available sign-in controls, two approved owners, least privilege, a guest/public-link review, connected-app approval, and restricted export storage. Official help states broad access can arise through workspace, teamspace, group, page, relation, or public-link routes, so inspect all grants.

## 18. Compliance and expert review

Obtain privacy/legal review before regulated, identity, health, employment, contract, or member-case content; records expertise before archive/compliance claims; identity/security review before SSO/SCIM or broad sharing; and migration expertise when critical databases/automations exceed manual reconciliation.

## 19. Integration boundary

No integration is catalog-verified. For each connected app record vendor, scopes, data read/written, owner, cost, failure behavior, and revocation. Keep billing, member, access, and other source systems authoritative; manually reconcile any pilot copy.

## 20. Configuration baseline

Record plan/billing term, owners, groups/teamspaces, page hierarchy, default/general access, guest policy, public-link state, owner/review properties, prohibited data, connected apps, AI/automation state, export formats/destination, recovery contacts, and prior fallback. Store no secrets in pages or this repository.

## 21. Smallest useful experiment

Create one private operations home, copy five non-critical SOPs, test one separate synthetic guest page, revoke the guest, export HTML and Markdown/CSV with files, and rebuild a navigation index on a clean machine.

## 22. Implementation sequence

1. Baseline source files, owners, attachments, and fallback.
2. Approve prohibited data, permissions, guests, and cost assumptions.
3. Configure a private minimal structure and least-privilege groups.
4. Copy five pages with source/owner/review metadata.
5. Test search, edit, denial, sharing, guest revocation, and recovery.
6. Export, reconcile, extract, and reconstruct the fallback index.
7. Obtain operations/privacy/export-review approval before expansion.

## 23. Migration and rollout

Copy rather than move pilot documents; freeze structural changes during acceptance; communicate the authoritative location; and migrate further content class by class only after an accepted export. Keep the prior store available until every required content type and fallback is proven.

## 24. Required test plan

Test all five pages and attachments, staff search/edit, unauthorized denial, absence of public links, guest isolation/revocation, owner/review visibility, account recovery for a non-owner, HTML and Markdown/CSV export, database fields, and clean fallback-index reconstruction. Record expected/actual result and evidence without sensitive content.

## 25. Acceptance criteria

All five expected pages are findable and owner-labeled; unauthorized and revoked users are denied; no internal public link exists; exported pages/files open; CSV fields are understood; omissions/losses are documented; and staff can use the reconstructed fallback. Any unexpected access or missing content blocks rollout.

## 26. Success metrics

Track time to find an approved SOP, overdue page reviews, permission/guest exceptions, public links found, export completeness, fallback reconstruction time, staff support effort, and annualized true cost. Do not claim staffing replacement or savings before measured evidence.

## 27. Monitoring and alerting

Monitor ownerless/stale pages, guests, public links, connected apps, account/recovery changes, failed exports, seat count/billing changes, and provider incidents affecting critical references. Every exception needs a named human and closure date.

## 28. Operating cadence

Monthly, review owners, stale pages, guests, public links, apps, seat count, and export result. Quarterly or after major structure/plan changes, reconstruct the fallback and review true cost. Re-test account recovery and critical SOP availability on an approved cadence.

## 29. Failure modes and response

Plan for SaaS/identity outage, accidental deletion, stale SOP, broad inherited permission, public/guest exposure, failed export, owner departure, and integration leakage. Use the read-only fallback during outage; freeze edits and remove access on exposure; preserve evidence and involve privacy/security owners.

## 30. Backup procedure

Inventory scoped pages/databases/files, export HTML and Markdown/CSV with files through an approved account, store the archive outside Notion under equal access controls, and reconcile counts. Official help notes inaccessible private pages and some teamspace-controlled content may be omitted, so record export account and omissions.

## 31. Restore drill

Notion states a workspace export cannot be instantly recreated by re-uploading it. Extract on a clean machine, open all pilot pages/files, reconcile five pages, inspect CSV columns, rebuild a navigation index, and record unsupported links/views/permissions/comments. No retained runtime restore evidence exists yet.

## 32. Upgrade, rollback, and exit

Treat plan, permission, AI, automation, integration, and workspace-structure changes as upgrades: verify official help, test non-sensitive pages, and export first. Roll back by freezing edits, exporting deltas, reconciling source files, removing guests/apps, and restoring the old index. Exit with a complete owner/content inventory, supported exports, field/link mapping, destination acceptance, account/app removal, billing closure, and available deletion confirmation.
