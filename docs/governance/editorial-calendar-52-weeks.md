# 52-Week Editorial Calendar

Planning window: 2026-08-03 through 2027-08-01  
Cadence: one planned operator content package per week  
Evidence status: planning only; no item is represented as researched, reviewed, tested, filmed, or
published

## Weekly content contract

Every entry has the same 14 required content fields: tool or comparison, operator problem, staff role,
task reduced, one-minute demo concept, three-to-five-minute video outline, pro tip, warning, newsletter
angle, LinkedIn angle, short social caption, call to action, related stack, and related workflow. Week is
an entry identifier, not an additional content field.

Demo and video concepts use fabricated data. Editors must verify current product, license, price,
privacy, security, export, accessibility, and integration details against official sources before
production. A concept is not runtime evidence or an endorsement.
