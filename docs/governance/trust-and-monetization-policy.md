# Trust, Editorial Independence, and Monetization Policy

Effective: 2026-07-18  
Applies to: catalog records, rankings, comparisons, guides, stacks, newsletters, social content,
events, and implementation material published as Open Coworking Stack

## Current posture

Open Coworking Stack is an independent, community-maintained resource. Paid placement in rankings is
not permitted. The project does not treat inclusion as endorsement, a current review date as complete
verification, or a vendor-provided claim as independent evidence.

This policy defines required disclosure even when the current value is "none." A future funding,
revenue, affiliate, consulting, gift, or vendor relationship must be disclosed before related content
is published; silence is not an acceptable disclosure.

## Required disclosures

The project must maintain a visible, dated disclosure covering all of the following:

| Topic                           | Required disclosure and control                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

Pseudonymous contributors may remain pseudonymous, but maintainers making conflicted decisions must disclose the conflict.                                                                      |

State "none" when applicable.                                                                                                  |

Sponsored content, if ever accepted, must be segregated and cannot alter catalog rankings.                                                                   |
| Affiliate and referral links    | Every tracked, commission-bearing, credit-bearing, or referral URL and the benefit recipient. A plain official source link is preferred. Affiliate relationships cannot affect conclusions.                                                                                                                    |
| Advertising and sponsorship     | Sponsor identity, cash or in-kind value category, dates, sponsored asset, editorial limits, and whether the sponsor saw content before publication. Ads must be visually labeled and separated from evidence.                                                                                                  |
| Vendor relationships            | Employment, investment, advisory roles, partnerships, reseller status, implementation work, free or discounted licenses, hosting credits, travel, gifts, early access, and private briefings involving a covered tool or competitor.                                                                           |

## Editorial rules

1. Operator usefulness, evidence quality, safety, portability, and total cost determine coverage.
2. Vendors do not buy inclusion, omission, rank, favorable language, verification, badges, backlinks,
   roadmap priority, or access to contributor identities.
3. Commercial and open-source tools use the same evidence standard. License class is factual context,
   not a quality score.
4. Comparisons disclose scope, operator profile, sources, date, unknowns, and conflicts. Missing facts
   remain unknown rather than receiving a neutral score.
5. Vendor copy, testimonials, usage counts, certifications, benchmarks, and pricing are attributed and
   dated. Self-asserted claims are not converted into independent findings.
6. Security reports follow the security policy. The project does not trade favorable coverage for
   vulnerability handling.
7. Recommendations identify human owners, data and professional boundaries, recurring labor, failure
   modes, exit options, and the smallest reversible experiment.
8. Fabricated examples are labeled as such. Anonymous operator stories require consent and enough
   context to avoid presenting one experience as universal evidence.

## Content-level disclosure block

Every sponsored, affiliate-linked, vendor-authored, gifted-access, or commercially conflicted asset
must place this block near the title, not only on this policy page:

```text
Relationship: [none or precise relationship]
Benefit: [recipient and cash/in-kind category]
Editorial control: [who selected scope, wrote, reviewed, and approved]
Evidence: [source and test types, dates, important unverified areas]
Links: [plain / affiliate / referral, and resulting benefit]
Conflicts and recusal: [none or recorded mitigation]
Last updated: [YYYY-MM-DD]
```

## Corrections and disputes

- Submit corrections through a repository issue or pull request with an official source, observed
  behavior, or reproducible evidence. Do not include personal data or undisclosed vulnerabilities.
- A maintainer acknowledges material factual corrections, records the disposition, and preserves a
  reviewable change in Git. There is no promise that promotional wording or unsupported claims will be
  accepted.
- Clearly harmful factual errors should be labeled or corrected promptly. Disputed evaluations remain
  published only with the disagreement and evidence boundary visible.
- Vendors may reply on the same terms as any contributor. A reply is not an editorial veto and is not
  hidden because the vendor declined sponsorship or services.

## Enforcement and change control

Undisclosed material conflicts can result in removal of links or claims, re-review, contributor
recusal, or reversal of a decision. Maintainers document policy exceptions and obtain an unconflicted
reviewer. Policy changes use a dated pull request and cannot retroactively erase prior relationships.
