# Professional-Help Boundaries

Last reviewed: 2026-07-18

Open Coworking Stack provides technical and operational starting points, not legal, accounting, tax,
security, privacy, accessibility, employment, insurance, real-estate, postal, or life-safety advice.
Software can organize evidence and reduce repetitive work; it cannot accept professional
responsibility or remove human approval.

## Stop and obtain qualified help

| Boundary                            | Stop before                                                                                                                                                          | Appropriate reviewer                                                                    | Evidence to retain                                                                                           |
| ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |

| Privacy and data protection         | Collecting identity evidence, video, biometrics, precise access history, health/accessibility details, employee monitoring, cross-border data, or novel AI profiling | Privacy counsel or qualified data-protection lead                                       | Purpose, lawful basis where applicable, minimization, recipients, retention, rights process, risk assessment |
| Postal and virtual-office services  | Receiving, scanning, forwarding, opening, destroying, or releasing mail; verifying a virtual-office customer                                                         | Postal/CMRA or local regulatory specialist and counsel                                  | Registration/authorization, identity procedure, custody log, release rules, retention, incident route        |
| Accounting and tax                  | Choosing a chart, tax treatment, invoice correction, revenue recognition, deposits, refunds, credits, write-offs, payroll, or statutory reports                      | Qualified accountant or tax adviser                                                     | Approved configuration, control totals, reconciliations, filing responsibility, exception approval           |
| Payments                            | Handling card data, changing payment flow, recurring mandates, disputes, refunds, or marketplace funds                                                               | Payment provider and PCI-qualified support as needed                                    | Scope diagram, provider attestation, no-card-data boundary, refund/dispute controls, access review           |

## Non-delegable human decisions

Do not allow a catalog tool, integration, or agent to make final decisions about member eligibility,
contract acceptance, pricing exceptions, credit, refunds, debt escalation, identity, consent, access
exceptions, emergency severity, employment, accommodations, legal retention, disclosure, or incident
closure. Automation may prepare a bounded recommendation or queue; a named person reviews the source
facts, records the decision, and can reverse or disable the automation.

## Escalation checklist

1. Stop the affected rollout or automation without destroying evidence.
2. Keep a safe manual service route and communicate limitations to affected people.
3. Record the question, jurisdiction, systems, data, affected people, deadline, and current owner.
4. Minimize any material shared with advisers and use an approved protected channel.
5. Obtain written advice or approval with assumptions and scope, not a vague verbal assurance.
6. Update configuration, procedures, retention, training, tests, and the decision log.
7. Re-test with synthetic data before resuming; obtain affected functional-owner approval.

## Selecting help

Verify jurisdiction, qualifications, relevant coworking/flexible-workspace experience, independence,
insurance, confidentiality, conflicts, deliverables, and who remains accountable after handoff. A
vendor implementation partner can explain its product but is not automatically independent legal,
financial, security, or accessibility assurance.
