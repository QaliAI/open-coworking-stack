# Coworking use cases

## Pilot use case

Receive member IT and facilities questions at one alias, assign a human owner, use reviewed response text, hand facilities cases to a named person/vendor path, and record a human closure rationale.

Measure source-mailbox versus queue counts, unassigned, overdue, escalated, reopened, and delivery failures. Metrics inform review; they do not decide staff performance or member fault.

## Exclusions

- Emergencies, life safety, and urgent access incidents use a separately staffed channel.
- Refunds, compensation, contractual disputes, and vendor spend require authorized people.
- Payment credentials and unnecessary sensitive attachments are rejected/redacted under policy.
