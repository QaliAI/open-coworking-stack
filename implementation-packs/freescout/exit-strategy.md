# Exit strategy

1. Inventory mailboxes, messages, attachments, contacts, status, tags, assignments, notes, agents, modules, and integrations actually used.
2. Freeze automation/rules and define a cutover timestamp.
3. Export or map records through verified supported paths; retain the underlying mailbox where policy permits.
4. Assign every open case and confirm its destination/owner in the replacement mailbox/system.
5. Redirect new mail, compare message counts, and keep a controlled read-only review window.
6. Revoke mailbox/app credentials, remove agents/modules, and dispose of data/backups under approved retention.

Export fidelity is unknown until tested, especially private notes, status, assignment, and attachments. Human owners approve reconciliation and destruction.
