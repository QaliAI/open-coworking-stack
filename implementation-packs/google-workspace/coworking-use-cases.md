# Coworking use cases

## Pilot use cases

- Store non-sensitive staff operating documents in function-owned shared drives rather than a departing person's area.
- Collect name, contact route, and a minimal tour request only where approved; assign each response to a human lead owner.
- Exercise a staff joiner/leaver and one approved guest-share lifecycle.

## Exclusions

Workspace is not the coworking CRM, billing ledger, contract decision-maker, or building-access system. Forms do not approve memberships, prices, refunds, identity, or access. Marketplace apps and CRM/archive/identity links require separate reviews.
