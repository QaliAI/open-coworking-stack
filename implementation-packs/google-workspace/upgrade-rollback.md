# Service change and rollback

Google controls SaaS releases; the operator controls configuration, plan, apps, integrations, and migrations. Before a material change, review current official notices/docs, export affected configuration/data where supported, identify affected groups/drives/forms/apps, and test critical sharing, offboarding, intake, and export flows with a small cohort.

Account, privacy/data, security, and business owners approve plan, app, retention, identity, sharing, or migration changes. Stop on permission expansion, ownership loss, form-routing failure, incompatible app behavior, or export/recovery failure.

Rollback means reversing the operator-controlled setting/app/migration where supported or returning to the preserved prior process. SaaS binaries cannot be operator-rolled back; document that evidence limit.
