# Pilot plan

1. Account owner records the purchased edition and admins; privacy/data owner records approved use and exclusions.
2. Create a small function group and shared drive with representative non-sensitive files.
3. Build one minimal tour form with a named human owner and controlled response destination.
4. Test staff access, blocked external share, one approved guest then revocation, and app-request rejection/approval workflow.
5. Test one synthetic leaver: suspend, transfer required ownership/data, revoke sessions/access, and review evidence.
6. Submit/delete synthetic tour responses and reconcile the lead handoff.
7. Export representative files, calendars/forms if used, and test usability/import without asserting full restore.
8. Exercise manual fallback; owners decide reject, revise, or separately scope rollout.

Stop for uncontrolled sharing, unknown ownership, excessive form data, unrecoverable admin access, unapproved apps, or failed export/fallback.
