# Security review

Review actual-edition administrator roles, strong authentication and recovery, groups, shared-drive permissions/ownership, external/link sharing, guest lifecycle, OAuth/marketplace grants, device/session considerations, form destinations, audit/security signals available to the subscription, support contacts, retention/deletion, exports, and incident response.

Record what was observed and tested in the operator's tenant. Documentation for another edition, vendor uptime, or a successful export does not prove independent backup, point-in-time restore, residency, certification, or legal compliance.

Use specialist help before federation, mail/domain migration, retention/eDiscovery, large permission changes, regulated data, or complex incident recovery. Privacy/legal owners decide monitoring, transfers, retention, and data-subject obligations.
