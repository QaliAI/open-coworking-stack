# Google Workspace coworking operator skill

## Coworking purpose

Give staff shared ownership of operational files and collect one minimal tour request with controlled access, handoff, offboarding, export, and fallback.

## Allowed automation

Deliver mail, enforce approved groups/settings, collect approved form fields, and notify the named lead owner. Use only controls observed in the operator's actual subscription.

## Prohibited decisions

Never decide prospect eligibility, price, contract/refund terms, building access, employment action, legal retention, external disclosure, or approval of an OAuth/marketplace app.

## Source verification

Use current official Admin Help, pricing, terms, and plan-specific admin screens. Do not infer Workspace controls from consumer products or another edition; label migration, residency, certification, support, and restore claims unverified.

## Data boundaries

Pilot with staff accounts, one shared-drive structure, groups, minimal prospect contact/request fields, and representative non-sensitive files. Exclude payments, identity documents, access credentials, HR, and unnecessary sensitive data.

## Human approvals

Account owner approves subscription/admins; domain owner approves DNS; privacy/data owner approves fields, sharing, retention, transfers, exports, and apps; managers approve staff access/leavers; a person reviews every lead and exceptional share.

## Required tests

Test named-admin recovery, sign-in, groups, shared ownership, blocked and approved guest sharing/revocation, app approval, form submission/deletion/handoff, leaver transfer/suspension, representative exports/imports, and manual fallback.

## Backup and rollback

Decide independent backup requirements separately from managed availability. Keep prior systems during pilot; stop intake, export/reconcile data, revoke guests/apps, and return staff to approved prior tools.

## Expert review

Use experienced Workspace/migration help for mail/domain cutover, bulk migration, federation, retention/eDiscovery, or permission restructuring; obtain privacy/legal review for monitoring, retention, transfers, requests, or regulated content.
