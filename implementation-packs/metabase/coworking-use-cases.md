# Coworking Use Cases

## Pilot

Display booked desk-hours divided by approved available desk-hours for one location and one reporting period. The metric contract decides treatment of cancellations, no-shows, closures, holds, and late changes.

## Possible follow-ons

- Meeting-room utilization by room and daypart.
- Tour inquiry trend from a separately governed aggregate source.
- Operations SLA trend where source timestamps and exclusions are approved.

Do not use this pack for member scoring, staff performance ranking, dynamic pricing, surveillance, or direct writes. Revenue and accounting dashboards require qualified finance review.
