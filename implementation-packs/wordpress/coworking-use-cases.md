# Coworking use cases

## Approved pilot

Private staging for minimum location, amenities, accessibility/contact, and tour pages with one minimal tour form. Synthetic requests go to a test recipient and are reconciled; the existing contact route remains available.

## Later, separately reviewed uses

- Publish coworking event or community content through an approval workflow.
- Maintain location-specific redirects and search metadata.
- Hand approved form fields to a CRM after a separate connector review.

## Out of scope

Membership/billing, card collection, access control, identity documents, unreviewed commerce/plugins, automated lead rejection/scoring, and unsupported compliance claims are not authorized.
