# WordPress coworking operator skill

## Coworking purpose

Publish accurate coworking location, amenities, access, and contact content and deliver one minimal tour request to named staff. WordPress supports publishing and capture; humans remain responsible for claims, accessibility, privacy, lead response, and operations.

## Allowed automation

- Build private staging and run approved link, availability, form, backup, update, security, performance, and accessibility checks.
- Deploy pre-approved content/dependency changes after a human initiates the release.
- Alert on form delivery, TLS, backup, storage, and supported update failures.
- Draft content and lead-delivery reports without publishing or qualifying leads.

## Prohibited decisions

- Do not publish claims, install/remove plugins/themes, change DNS, or perform major updates without approval.
- Do not add form fields, analytics, marketing recipients, payment capture, or third-party embeds autonomously.
- Do not qualify/reject leads, send marketing, delete submissions, or decide privacy/accessibility compliance.
- Do not expose staging, backups, credentials, or real lead data in tests.

## Source verification

Record core, runtime, host, theme, and every plugin version; recheck [official WordPress documentation](https://wordpress.org/documentation/), the [core repository](https://github.com/WordPress/WordPress), and each dependency's official source/terms/advisories. The 2026-07-17 core snapshot does not verify host, plugin, form, email, accessibility, support, or recovery behavior.

## Data boundaries

Allow approved public content/media, named staff users, minimum tour fields, form delivery metadata, and required site/database/configuration state. Prohibit payment/card data, identity documents, door credentials, member billing records, unnecessary sensitive/free-text fields, and real leads in staging.

## Human approvals

Marketing approves content/claims; privacy approves form fields, retention, third parties, and notices; accessibility approves launch evidence; security approves dependencies/exposure; technical/change owners approve deployment/rollback; the lead owner approves delivery and fallback. Renew approvals for themes, plugins, forms, DNS, or integrations.

## Required tests

Test responsive content, keyboard/focus/labels/errors/contrast/zoom and representative assistive technology, links/redirects, roles, unauthorized access, form success/validation/spam/provider failure/duplicates, email and fallback, performance, backup restore, content export, updates, security controls, and rollback. Reconcile synthetic submissions end to end.

## Backup and rollback

Protect a consistent database, uploads, approved themes/plugins, and required configuration off-host with version/dependency records. Restore only to isolated staging and prevent delivery to real recipients. Keep previous deployment/DNS available; rollback must reconcile content and leads created during the change window.

## Expert review

Require security expertise for custom code, complex plugins, compromise, internet-facing admin changes, or high-value traffic; accessibility expertise before launch/major redesign; privacy/legal review for forms, analytics, marketing, or legal claims; and database/hosting expertise for major migrations or strict recovery objectives.
