# Upgrade and rollback

## Before change

Read release/advisory/compatibility notes for core, runtime, theme, and each plugin. Remove unsupported dependencies, take a full backup, restore a privacy-safe copy, and update one attributable group at a time in staging.

## Test and release

Run content, redirect, role, form, email, accessibility, performance, security, scheduled-task, and backup checks. Obtain technical and business approval before production and monitor synthetic lead delivery after release.

## Stop and rollback

Stop for site/admin errors, security alert, broken form/delivery, critical accessibility regression, redirect loss, or unacceptable performance. Restore the tested prior compatible files/database or previous deployment/DNS route, then reconcile content and every lead from the change window.
