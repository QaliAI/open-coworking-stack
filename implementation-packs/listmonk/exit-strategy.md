# Exit Strategy

Freeze campaigns and audience changes. Export subscriber addresses, status, list membership, and approved consent reference; retain an independent suppression file. Archive required campaign content/evidence and verify representative exports.

Import a suppressed and an active fixture into the replacement before any live data. Reconcile counts and status, then revoke SMTP/API credentials and bounce webhooks, remove public/private routes, disable accounts, and delete listmonk data/backups under policy.

Unknown until tested: full campaign/template portability, metric portability, and status mapping to a chosen replacement. Do not send from the replacement until the privacy owner accepts that mapping.
