# Coworking Use Cases

## Start here

Send a monthly operations update to staff and advisors who explicitly agreed to the test. Then evaluate one member event announcement using an approved event-interest list.

## Later, with a new review

- Location-specific planned-maintenance notices where list membership and fallback are controlled.
- Opt-in community newsletters with separately approved tracking and retention.
- Event follow-up to attendees whose consent covers that message.

## Out of scope

Emergency or transactional notices, invoices, access messages, purchased lists, inferred interests, disciplinary notices, and any campaign where delayed or failed SMTP delivery creates safety or contractual harm.
