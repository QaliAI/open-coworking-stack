# Coworking Use Cases

## First use

Two operations staff maintain one non-sensitive opening/closing SOP and hand off an edited copy without email attachments.

## Controlled later uses

- Staff templates and approved brand-neutral member handout masters.
- Short-lived member file exchange with named owner and expiry.
- Shared operations calendar only after separate client/export/restore tests.
- Form intake only after destination, response access, retention, and export are approved.

Exclude billing, contracts requiring legal record controls not yet designed, access credentials, CCTV, identity documents, health/accommodation records, disputes, and HR files from the pilot.
