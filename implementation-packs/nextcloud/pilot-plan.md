# Staff Folder Pilot

## Scope

One folder, two active staff, one leaver/denied fixture, three neutral files, and no extra app or identity integration.

## Run

Upload, sync, edit, rename, and recover the fixtures under the agreed retention. Test simultaneous edits and preserve both conflict copies. Remove one active user from the pilot group and prove access closes on web and client. Fill a staging storage quota and interrupt database access to observe safe failure.

## Recovery

Export neutral files and compare hashes. Take a coordinated backup and restore it to isolation. Verify content, group access, denied user, share inventory, and background jobs.

## Pass gate

No unintended access or data loss, leaver closure is timely, users understand conflict handling, exports open independently, capacity alerts work, and restore evidence is accepted.
