# Coworking Use Cases

## Evaluation scenario

Move ten synthetic inquiries through `New`, `Contacted`, `Proposal review`, and `Won/Lost`, then create a synthetic customer/product/invoice sample for finance to assess workflow shape.

## Questions the evaluation may answer

- Can sales users follow the agreed stages without administrative access?
- Can duplicates and External IDs be controlled during migration rehearsal?
- Are required CRM and invoice sample fields actually present in this Community version?
- Can records and attachments be recovered and exported in useful form?

It cannot approve production accounting, tax, recurring billing, payments, bookings, door access, member portals, e-signature, helpdesk, or any Enterprise/add-on capability.
