# Chatwoot pilot plan

## Design

Complete `templates/inbox-policy.yaml` and publish the privacy, operating-hours, and urgent-contact wording. Select a staging or limited website page and two trained agents.

## Test

Run agent/unauthorized access, visitor reply/private note, assignment/reassignment, labels, attachment, after-hours, notification, closure/reopen, escalation, deletion/export, credential revocation, and outage-fallback cases.

## Operate

Handle limited enquiries for one week. At each operating period, inspect unassigned/stale conversations and reconcile sales/facilities handoffs. Log sensitive-data incidents without copying transcript content into this repository.

## Decide

Inbox, privacy, website, after-hours, and technical owners review `examples/pilot-scorecard.csv`. Expansion requires recovery evidence and does not authorize another channel. All behavior is currently untested.
