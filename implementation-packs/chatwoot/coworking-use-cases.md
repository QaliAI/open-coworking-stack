# Coworking use cases

## Website enquiry triage

Classify an incoming enquiry under approved labels and assign a named agent. The agent reads context and chooses the response; labels do not determine eligibility or urgency automatically.

## Sales handoff

Record a minimum handoff summary and owner, then confirm receipt in the approved CRM/manual queue. Do not silently treat a label as completed follow-up.

## Facilities escalation

Move operational context to the named facilities route without promising resolution. Safety/emergency reports use the published urgent path, not an unattended inbox.

## After hours

Show honest operating hours, expected response, and urgent alternative. Test what users actually see; no bot should imply a human is present.

Email, social, WhatsApp, bots, and help center are untested and excluded.
