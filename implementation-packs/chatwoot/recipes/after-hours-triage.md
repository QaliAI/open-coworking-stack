# Recipe: after-hours queue handoff

1. At opening, inspect unassigned, open, pending, and high-age conversations; do not rely on notifications alone.
2. Check for language indicating safety or urgent facilities risk and route it to the published human process; do not diagnose automatically.
3. Assign every actionable conversation to a named agent or team under the approved rules.
4. Use a private note only for minimum factual handoff context; never put a public response in a private-note field by mistake.
5. Confirm sales/facilities receipt outside Chatwoot where the handoff requires it.
6. Record missed-notification or after-hours defects and keep the conversation open until ownership is accepted.

This recipe does not make emergency, membership, refund, or complaint decisions.
