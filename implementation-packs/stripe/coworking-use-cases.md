# Coworking use cases

## In scope

- Payment request for an already approved coworking invoice.
- Correlation of invoice, payment, fee, refund/dispute, and payout records.
- Human follow-up for decline or exception.
- Sandbox testing of a documented connector and verified webhook handling.

## Out of scope

- Member/contract/accounting source of truth.
- Price, tax, discount, credit, write-off, refund, dispute, or fraud decisions.
- Raw card details in operator forms, servers, logs, tickets, or examples.
- Payment status directly granting a door credential or emergency access.

Use a provider-hosted/low-risk flow and qualified review rather than inventing card collection.
