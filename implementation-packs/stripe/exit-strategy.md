# Exit strategy

1. Inventory customers, payment methods/tokens, invoices, subscriptions, payments, refunds, disputes, balances, payouts, fees, metadata, keys, and retention duties.
2. Confirm supported exports/API access and which payment credentials/mandates are non-portable or require recollection.
3. Freeze creation/cut over new invoices at an approved time while maintaining open-case access.
4. Export and reconcile totals/counts to the accounting ledger.
5. Build and test destination ID/status mappings; communicate member action where recollection is required.
6. Resolve or transfer open refunds/disputes under provider/legal rules.
7. Revoke endpoints/keys/users and close only after finance, legal, and account-owner approval.

Never promise token portability before both providers and applicable rules confirm it.
