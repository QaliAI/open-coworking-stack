# Exit strategy

Maintain a vendor-neutral inventory of device models, serials, locations, cables, PoE, VLANs, SSIDs, IP ranges, DHCP/DNS, firewall intent, admin/recovery custody, ISP dependencies, and physical replacement constraints.

Before ecosystem expansion, estimate replacement hardware, cabling, labor, outage windows, and support. Test extraction of required operational records; do not treat a proprietary controller backup as a neutral migration format.

For exit, stage the replacement in parallel, validate coverage/isolation/member workflows, obtain cutover approval, preserve incident evidence under policy, revoke controller/cloud access, reset or dispose of hardware securely, and delete retained client data when required.
