# Coworking use cases

## Pilot: staff access to one internal app

Use an approved group/policy to authenticate two named staff users to a non-critical app. The app owner, not Authentik, owns authorization correctness.

## Joiner

Create access only from an approved roster/request, enroll MFA, and have the app owner confirm effective privilege. Never infer access from job title alone.

## Leaver

At the manager-approved time, disable identity access, verify new login is denied, assess existing sessions, reconcile the app, and record any exception.

## Recovery

Use the independently held break-glass route and witnessed MFA recovery process. Emergency access must be reviewed after use.

Member identity, guest Wi-Fi, door access, and regulated systems are untested and excluded.
