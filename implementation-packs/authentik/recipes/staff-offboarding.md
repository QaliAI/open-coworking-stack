# Recipe: witnessed staff offboarding

1. Confirm identity and effective time from the approved leaver record.
2. Have the identity operator disable the pilot account; do not delete it first.
3. Verify a new app login is denied and document existing-session behavior.
4. Have the app owner reconcile direct/local accounts, groups, tokens, and privileged sessions.
5. Revoke recovery/API material owned by the leaver through the relevant system.
6. Record exceptions, evidence, retention decision, and manager/app-owner sign-off.

This recipe does not decide employment status or offboarding time.
