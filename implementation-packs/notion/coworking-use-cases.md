# Coworking use cases

## In scope

- Private staff operations home with five named SOP owners.
- Non-critical opening, closing, tour, room-reset, and escalation reference pages.
- Lightweight action tracker whose authoritative owner and manual fallback are documented.
- Separate contractor test page with one time-bounded guest.

## Out of scope

- Member, contract, billing, accounting, access-control, incident, or legal-record systems.
- Public publishing of internal operations content.
- AI deciding or publishing operational instructions.
- Workflows that cannot continue from the prior document store.

## Success signal

Staff retrieve the approved page, denied users remain denied, the test guest is revoked, and the five-page fallback can be reconstructed from inspected exports.
