# Change and rollback

## Change gate

Treat plan changes, workspace restructuring, new permission models, AI, forms, automations, and connected apps as changes. Verify official help, test on non-sensitive pages, review scopes/cost, and take a fresh export first.

## Rollback triggers

- Unexpected access or public exposure.
- Missing/unreadable content or attachment.
- Failed export or account recovery.
- Unacceptable cost, governance labor, or staff confusion.

## Procedure

Freeze edits, export changed content, reconcile it to source files, remove guests and connections, restore the prior staff index, communicate the authoritative location, and record privacy/operations approval. Do not delete the pilot until retention and evidence needs are satisfied.
