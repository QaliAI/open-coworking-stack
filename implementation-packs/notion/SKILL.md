# Notion coworking operations skill

## Coworking purpose

Help staff find and maintain approved non-critical opening, closing, tour, room-reset, and escalation SOPs in a private operations home.

## Allowed automation

Create review reminders, flag missing owners, prepare a guest/public-link review, and inventory export files. Draft outputs remain pending until a named owner approves them.

## Prohibited decisions

Do not publish SOPs, grant guests, enable public links or integrations, decide membership/access/refunds/safety responses, delete source records, or claim an export is a restorable workspace.

## Source verification

Use current official Notion help and pricing pages. Record URL, access date, applicable plan, and unresolved limits. Mark local behavior untested until evidence exists.

## Data boundaries

Use only the five approved SOP copies and synthetic guest test content. Exclude payment data, credentials, door codes, member cases, identity/health data, contracts, and unreviewed AI inputs.

## Human approvals

The SOP owner approves content; privacy approves data scope; the workspace owner approves access; operations approves rollout; and an independent reviewer signs the export/fallback test.

## Required tests

Test search, edit, unauthorized denial, public-link absence, one guest's limited access and revocation, owner/review metadata, HTML and Markdown/CSV export, attachments, and fallback-index reconstruction.

## Backup and rollback

Keep source files unchanged, create protected exports outside Notion, reconcile expected content, and roll back by freezing edits, exporting deltas, removing guests/connections, and restoring the old staff index.

## Expert review

Require privacy/legal review for regulated or sensitive content, identity expertise for SSO/SCIM, records expertise for archive claims, and migration expertise for critical databases or automations.
