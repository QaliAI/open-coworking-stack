# Recipe: 20-desk floor pilot

1. Label 20 physical desks and enter the exact same IDs in the independent register.
2. Upload an approved floor image and place each desk; facilities checks onsite.
3. Configure approved hours, timezone, booking duration/count, cancellation, and any designated approval.
4. Create synthetic admin, eligible member, ineligible member, and disabled-user cases.
5. Test conflicts, cancellation, approval, denied access, mobile layout, and timezone.
6. Restore the complete staging state to clean infrastructure and repeat a sample booking.
7. Import only the approved pilot roster and run for 14 days with manual fallback.
8. Aggregate results; operations, privacy, facilities, and community staff decide whether to expand.

Do not collect payment or connect bookings to doors during this recipe.
