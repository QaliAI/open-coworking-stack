# Coworking use cases

## In scope

- Searchable archive pilot for 20 historical supplier invoices.
- Human-reviewed OCR, title/date, correspondent, document type, and tags.
- Controlled finance access and an explicit denied-user test.
- Export/import, sanity-check, backup, upgrade, rollback, and exit evidence.

## Out of scope

- Virtual mailroom or unattended email/scanner intake.
- Member mail, contracts, identity/health files, legal hold, or payment processing.
- Original destruction based only on scanning/OCR success.
- Automatic matching, hooks, or duplicate deletion without reviewed rules and human checks.

Use a qualified mailroom/records service when chain of custody or regulated handling exceeds operator capability.
