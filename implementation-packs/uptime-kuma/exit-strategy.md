# Exit strategy

Maintain a vendor-neutral register of targets, check types, expected responses, safe intervals, delays, maintenance windows, service owners, runbooks, escalation contacts, notification routes, status text, and retention.

Before exit, recreate checks on the replacement and run old and new systems in parallel through approved controlled failures. Compare detection, delivery, acknowledgement, and recovery; separately complete real member workflows.

After approval, preserve required incident summaries, revoke notification/check tokens, remove public status routes, delete application data/backups under policy, retire DNS/proxy/host access, and confirm every service still has a manual owner. Export fidelity remains unverified until tested.
