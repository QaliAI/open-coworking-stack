# Coworking use cases

## Approved pilot

Count staging views of one tour landing page and one synthetic CTA event. Marketing compares the aggregate signal with separately governed booking/CRM totals; no lead-level join occurs.

## Later, separately reviewed uses

- Compare aggregate traffic to published location or event pages.
- Detect that approved website analytics collection stopped.
- Produce a retention-limited aggregate marketing report.

## Out of scope

Member tracking, employee monitoring, CRM identity attribution, visitor fingerprinting, free-text event values, automated campaign changes, and legal conclusions are not authorized by this pack.
