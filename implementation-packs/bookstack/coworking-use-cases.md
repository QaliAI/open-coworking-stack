# Coworking use cases

## Opening and closing

Publish a location-specific checklist with owner, approved date, prerequisites, escalation, and exception handling. Do not include alarm/access secrets.

## Emergency reference

Publish only after qualified review, identify last review and offline fallback, and make clear that the page does not replace training or emergency services.

## Member FAQ

Separate approved public wording from private operational notes. Test with a guest/unauthorized session before publication.

## Multi-site runbook

Use an approved base procedure plus explicit local variation and owner. Do not silently overwrite site-specific requirements.

All page accuracy and permission behavior are untested until the pilot evidence is completed.
