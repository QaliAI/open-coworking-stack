# BookStack exit strategy

1. Freeze edits and export a page/content register with owners, classes, review dates, and source links.
2. Export pages/books in supported formats and separately archive mapped images/attachments.
3. Reconcile content changed since source migration; resolve conflicts with page owners.
4. Import or recreate permissions in the replacement and test private denial/public visibility.
5. Keep old BookStack read-only until critical pages, links, and attachments are accepted.
6. Redirect staff and preserve an offline emergency set.
7. Remove auth/webhooks, revoke secrets, and retire accounts/backups under retention approval.

Export/import fidelity, comments, revisions, links, and permission portability are untested and may require manual migration.
