# Recipe: publish an owned SOP

1. Match the draft to an authoritative source in the content register.
2. Add purpose, scope, prerequisites, ordered steps, exceptions, escalation, owner, reviewer, approval date, and next review.
3. Remove secrets and unnecessary personal data; review every attachment/link.
4. Apply the approved shelf/book and role boundary.
5. Have a non-author reviewer walk the procedure; obtain specialist review if safety/legal/privacy applies.
6. Test as reader and unauthorized user, then have the knowledge owner publish.
7. Preserve the superseded source/read-only page according to retention and notify affected staff.

Publication does not prove safety or legal compliance.
