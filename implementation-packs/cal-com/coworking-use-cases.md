# Coworking use cases

## Tour booking

Offer only times the host has approved, collect minimum contact and preparation information, and retain a visible manual/accessibility route.

## Reschedule or cancellation

Allow the configured path only when messages, cutoffs, calendar updates, and staff ownership have been tested. Staff handle exceptions and affected visitor communication.

## No-show follow-up

Create a staff task or queue entry under an approved rule; do not classify the lead or send indefinite marketing automatically.

## Later evaluations

Room inventory, events, day passes, payments, routing, and access control require separate owner, risk, data, and acceptance plans. Tour-pilot success is not evidence for them.
