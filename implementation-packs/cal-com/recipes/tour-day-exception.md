# Recipe: tour-day exception

1. Verify the booking and attendee through the approved contact data; do not request identity documents in chat/email by default.
2. Check the authoritative host calendar and site operating status.
3. If host/location is unavailable, assign a staff owner and offer approved alternatives manually.
4. Record the reschedule/cancellation through the tested path and confirm both calendar and attendee messages.
5. Escalate accessibility, safety, complaint, or contractual issues to the named human owner.
6. Reconcile the booking, CRM/manual follow-up, and visitor-arrival list.

Automation must not promise access, price, or accommodation during an exception.
