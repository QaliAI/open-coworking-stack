# Exit strategy

1. Freeze scope and inventory customers, items, invoices, payments metadata, accounts, taxes, statuses, files, users/roles, apps, and custom fields actually used.
2. Define target fields and authoritative source for each object.
3. Export representative and full approved datasets through supported paths; preserve identifiers.
4. Finance reconciles counts, totals, open balances, statuses, and statutory records independently.
5. Process owner tests the replacement/current workflow including corrections and open work.
6. Keep ERPNext read-only for the approved retention period, then revoke access and dispose of data/backups under policy.

Customizations and jurisdictional requirements can reduce portability. Professional migration and accounting review are required before production exit; this pack proves none.
