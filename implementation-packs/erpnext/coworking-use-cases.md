# Coworking use cases

## Pilot use case

Represent one approved membership product as an item, create one synthetic customer, produce a draft invoice, review the configured totals/statuses, record synthetic payment metadata, exercise correction/cancellation, and export the result.

## Later, separately governed evaluations

- Asset/inventory tracking for coworking equipment.
- Procurement or supplier workflows.
- Broader customer/accounting consolidation.

Each expands data, control, localization, and expertise requirements. This pack does not approve them.

## Exclusions

ERPNext must not decide discounts, refunds, credit, tax, contractual eligibility, staff actions, or physical access. Production accounting and HR are out of scope.
